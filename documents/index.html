What it is meant to do

  • Attach files to checklist rows. A row is only really "received" when the PDF is attached, so upload belongs on the item, not in a separate folder tree.
  • Give the advisor a download. A single, expiring link per tax year — the advisor never needs an account on the family's site.
  • Keep an audit trail. Who uploaded what and when, so a missing document is a question with an answer.
  • Stop accepting uploads once the year is final. Same lock as the checklist.

Why it is not here yet

File upload is the first feature that genuinely cannot be faked in the browser: it needs storage (Cloudflare R2), authentication, and a considered retention policy for what is, by definition, a pile of sensitive financial documents. That work is sequenced after the checklist backend — see the roadmap.
